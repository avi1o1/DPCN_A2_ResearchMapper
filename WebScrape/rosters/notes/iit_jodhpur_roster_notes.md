# IIT Jodhpur faculty roster - lab notes

Output: `rosters/iit_jodhpur_faculty.csv` (443 rows: 324 current, 74 former, 44 adjunct, 1 visiting). Built by a continuation agent after an earlier agent was lost to a usage limit (it had downloaded the NIRF PDFs, unit list pages and CDX lists). Scratch data and parsers: `<scratchpad>/work/iit_jodhpur_roster/{data,code}`.

Profile URL = `/People/Profile/<uuid>`, e-mail from the card. The live host raises an SSL error in the fetcher (`DEAD:SSLError`), so only the archive copy is used.
   Adjunct / visiting / professor-of-practice lists: same endpoint with category GUIDs (adj `f3e9fe15-...`, vis `5d7f9d14-...`/`05491c37-...`), 35 cards. ## Independent size check
NIRF 2026 submission (Feb 2026, data year 2024-25): 269 faculty rows flagged "currently working" (267 distinct people after de-duplicating Eng/Overall) versus **324 current rows** here. 267 current rows are NIRF-confirmed (all of them); 57 current rows are on the 2026 website lists but not in the NIRF tables (joined or listed after the data year; the lists are from May-June 2026), 41 rows lack a People/List source (NIRF-only people: 43 NIRF-current people are not on the website lists, e.g. most of EE - the archived EE page shows 17 names whereas the AR2425 reports 36 EE faculty, so the archived web pages are incomplete). Difference 324-269 = 55 is explained by the 57 post-data-year joiners / adjunct-type listings, minus duplicates. AR2425 department counts (EE 36, ...) are consistent with the roster.

## How former faculty and years were established
Former = person in any NIRF submission 2017-2026 who is not "currently working" with no leaving date in the 2026 files. LAST = year of the leaving date when given (all 2021+ files carry them); otherwise the last submission year that listed the person as current (flagged per person below as "last NIRF appearance"). FIRST = earliest joining date in any NIRF year (NIRF dates can be regularisation dates; the earliest across years is used). People whose leaving year is before 2016 were dropped. Names of former people are kept exactly as NIRF printed them; NIRF mixes "Surname Given" and "Given Surname" so a few rows are surname-first (e.g. "Bhattacharya Rima"); no re-ordering was attempted because it was found unreliable.
joining_year: NIRF earliest joining year only (344 rows). Rows without it (site-only recent joiners, adjuncts) are blank.
Visiting rows: persons whose NIRF association type was "Visiting" in every year (one former). Designations that NIRF gives only as category ("Other", "Associate Dean", "Dean/...") are replaced by an earlier plain rank when one exists, else "Faculty (NIRF category: X)".

## Problems / gaps (known)
1. **Former-faculty coverage is limited.** Old site rosters (`iitj.ac.in/faculty/index.php?id=<dept>`) are hiring/specialisation pages, and `department/index.php?rec=faculty_member&dept=<d>` snapshots (2017-2024, 11 units fetched) contain no names (the list is loaded dynamically), so a unit-by-unit Wayback roster per year could not be built. Former faculty therefore come from NIRF only (mostly Engineering up to 2021, Overall from 2021): sciences/humanities/management faculty who left before ~2021 and were never in a NIRF table are missing. Annual-report candidates (e.g. names under AR "Faculty Members" tables in 2015-2020) were not added because staff and faculty could not be separated reliably.
2. Wayback was unreachable for a period (connection refused); a few requests were retried.
3. Department is blank for 52 rows (10 current NIRF-only, 29 former, adjunct-from-snapshot) because no AR/ORCID source gave a unit.
4. 43 current NIRF-only people have department taken from AR/ORCID votes where available (note per conflict not kept in CSV); verify against the live site.
5. NIRF 2026 "current" status is a Feb 2026 statement; leavers after that, and lists dated May-June 2026, may be stale by Oct 2026.
6. ORCID lookup via the live API not run (offline index only).
7. Ajay Agarwal: NIRF joining 2021 but the annual report names him as EE professor with "tenure up to 31.08.2024" in an office; joining year left as NIRF gave it.
