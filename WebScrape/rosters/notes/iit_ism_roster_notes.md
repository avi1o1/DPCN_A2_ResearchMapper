## How people, tenure and status were established
* Person universe = site list + every NIRF row (10 editions) + old Wayback lists, merged by name clustering (`cluster.py`): normalised tokens (titles, "Kr"=Kumar, Md/Mohd/Mohammad unified), exact token-set > fuzzy tokens (>=0.88) > initials > subset names; guards: two rows from the same source/edition never merge; fuzzy/subset links must have NIRF joining dates within 1 year; initials never combined with fuzzy tokens. 765 clusters before the one site duplicate was removed.
* `joining_year`: NIRF joining date year (earliest across editions) for 708 rows; profile page (own position table) for 8 site people not in NIRF; blank 39 (current site people with no NIRF row and no readable date, mostly 2026 joiners; and two NIRF-2016-only people). Profile dates were NOT used to override NIRF: my automatic reading of the Position tab picked the next (older) entry's date for 10 of 14 disagreements (checked by hand), so NIRF dates stand. Manual: Tusharkanti Dey 2019 (own page: Visiting Assistant Professor at IIT (ISM) Nov 2019, NIRF says 2020); Arkadeb Pal 2026 (Ramanujan Fellow 2026-present; auto-read 2024 was a MSCA fellowship elsewhere); Suparno Bhattacharyya 2025 and Indumathy J 2025 (own page). Caveat: NIRF joining dates can be regularisation dates (e.g. promotions to Grade-I are not joining dates, but some people show an earlier profile appointment); 14 site people have a profile year that differs from NIRF, not resolved further.
* Former = person present in some NIRF edition or old list but not on the site list now. FIRST = NIRF joining year (earliest) else first evidence year (for 2016-edition-only people); LAST = last evidence year, where an edition y counts as evidence for year y-1 (submissions in late y-1/early y; check: max joining date in edition 2025 is Dec 2024, in 2024 is Jul 2023), the 2016 edition counts as 2016, an old snapshot as its year, and a NIRF leaving date (only trusted when the row's currently-working flag is No) as its year. People last seen in NIRF 2026 therefore get LAST=2025. Uncertainty +-1 year everywhere; the missing 2017 edition means people who left in 2017 are dated by the 2016/2018 editions and Wayback 2017 pages.
* Window: 9 people with all evidence before 2016 (NIRF rows with leaving dates 2014-2015, e.g. Nirendra M Mishra, Parboti Shankar Mukherjee, Vinaya K Srivastava) are excluded.
* Status for non-listed people: NIRF category "Adhoc/Contractual/Other" in their latest edition -> `visiting` with designation "Re-employed/ad hoc/contractual (NIRF category Other) (Y1-Y2)" (decision matching BHU/Mandi; 178 rows, typically re-employed retired professors and contractual faculty); "Visiting/Emeritus/Adjunct" in an old snapshot -> that status; otherwise `former`. Mixed people (regular earlier, re-employed later) follow their latest category.
* Departments of non-listed people: only from the 2017/2022/2023 Wayback lists (97 of 309 non-site rows); the other 212 (154 visiting, 58 former) have **blank department** because NIRF has no department column. Old department names mapped: Applied Chemistry/Chemistry -> Chemistry and Chemical Biology; Applied Mathematics -> Mathematics & Computing; Applied Physics -> Physics; Environmental Engineering -> Environmental Science and Engineering; Mineral Engineering -> Fuel, Minerals and Metallurgical Engineering; Mining Machinery Engineering -> "Mining Machinery Engineering (defunct)" (3 former).
* Tie-break: site list wins over NIRF. 12 people marked "currently working" in NIRF 2026 (data to mid-2025) but absent from the site are therefore `former` with LAST=2025: Debashree Roy, Rahul M R, Nirmal Kumar Singh, Om Prakash Singh, Srinivas Pasupuleti, Nabakumar Jana, Banshidhar Majhi, Diti Goswami, Dharmananda Sarangi, Prasanta K. Jana, Manoj Kumar, Ramakrishna R Sonde. Some may be omissions on the website, or left after mid-2025 (Banshidhar Majhi / Om Prakash Singh / Debashree Roy / Dharmananda Sarangi / Manoj Kumar / Ramakrishna Sonde joined 2024-25). Six site people hold NIRF "Adhoc" in 2026 yet are on the site as listed faculty: kept per site designation.

## Ambiguities and problems
* Repeated names: Ashok Kumar (2 current + 1-2 former), Rakesh Kumar, Jitendra Kumar, Manoj Kumar: clusters separated by NIRF joining date and site order; history rows for these names may be mixed (20 ties logged during clustering, all of this kind).
* NIRF name wrapping: pdftotext wraps long names; single-token rows (JHASAKETAN) were linked by exact token + same joining date. Name spelling variants merged by alias: Dhurva/Dhruva Kumar Singh, L A Kumaraswamidhas, G N Singh = Garib Nath Singh, V G Villuri, Jhasakentan Nayek = Jhasaketan Nayak. "Pradip Sircar" (Visiting Professor, 2022-23 Wayback lists only) is a separate `visiting` row.
* NIRF 2019 contained page-break artifacts (URL text inside names): names cut at the URL, row kept. 2018/2019 leaving dates wrap across lines and were mis-read for several people; leaving dates are accepted only when currently-working = No and not earlier than joining.
* No second-unit cases could be derived (site lists each person once). Professors on deputation as Director elsewhere (CIMFR, IIPE, IIEST) are kept `current` with the printed designation.

## Known gaps
* No department for 212 non-listed rows; no former-faculty list on the site. 2017 NIRF edition absent; Wayback has no department lists for 2018-2021, so people who joined and left between NIRF editions (about 1-3 years) are missed (NIRF is also an annual snapshot of an extra-large "Other" population).
* NIRF rows for Adhoc/Other people can include persons who are not faculty in the strict sense (re-employed staff, project/ad hoc teachers); the brief's decision (keep as visiting) was followed.
* Tenure years have +-1 year uncertainty (see above); joining years of 39 rows unknown.
