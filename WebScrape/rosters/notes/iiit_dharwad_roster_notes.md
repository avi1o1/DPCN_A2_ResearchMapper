## Sources and endpoints used
1. NIRF data submissions (Overall report, faculty table with joining date, "currently working", leaving date, association type): 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 (Wayback; Engineering reports carry the same table). Parsed with `pdftotext -raw`. The NIRF 2024 table is garbled by the PDF layout (columns shifted): only its names are used (32 names), never its dates. NIRF 2019-2021 are not published on the site/archive.

Wayback snapshots of the old and new faculty pages (CDX `iiitdwd.ac.in/faculty*`, `/people*`, `/department*`, `/visiting-faculty*`): `faculty.php` (2016-10), WordPress `/faculty/` (2016-12, 2017-12, 2018-06), `/faculty/` (2019-10), `Faculty.php` (2020-09, 2021-07, 2021-12, 2022-10, 2023-12), `/faculty/` (2024-11), `/academics/faculty/` (2025-05); `/visiting-faculty/` (2016-12, 2018-06, 2019-09); `/director/former/` (2025-03). ## How former faculty and tenure years were established
Evidence years: annual report N-N+1 counted as the year of its as-on date (AR 2015-17 as 2016/2017; AR 2017-18 as 2018 because it footnotes "joined July 2018"; AR 2018-19 as 2019; 2019-20 as 2020; 2020-21 as 2021; 2021-22 as 2022); Wayback snapshot = year of capture; NIRF 2022 = 2022, NIRF 2023 and 2024 = 2023, NIRF 2025 = 2024.
