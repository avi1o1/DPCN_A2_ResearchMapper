# IIT Mandi roster notes (iit_mandi)

Built 2026-10-28 by one agent (resumed after two pauses). Scripts/caches: scratchpad `work/iit_mandi_roster/code/` (nirf_parse.py, lib.py, cluster.py, wbparse.py, wbfetch.py, facpages.py, build.py, post.py) and `.../data/`. Reused from the golden-set agent: `iit_mandi_golden/data/allfaculty.html` (live directory, fetched 2026-10-26), faculty-page caches `fac/` (for identifiers only). All fetches through the shared fetcher (`https://www.iitmandi.ac.in` / `https://iitmandi.ac.in`; `http://` hung); no bot walls met. The annual reports 2016-17..2024-25 were searched for joined/left lists and for faculty tables: they contain no usable faculty lists (several are image-only; the others only school descriptions, committees, staff "relieved" lists), so they were not used as a source for rows.

## How status, tenure and years were established
* current = on the live directory (Oct 2026), or on a school page, or on the 2026-06 archived directory (Amit Sil only). Designation Adjunct / Professor of Practice / Distinguished Professor -> `adjunct`; Visiting / Guest / DST-Inspire / Teaching Professor -> `visiting`.
* NIRF-only people: NIRF 2026 "currently working = Yes" and Regular -> current (Behera, Kadiyam, Naik, R. K. Yadav, Sethi); Contractual / "Other" -> visiting ("Visiting/contractual faculty (NIRF designation Other)"; the NIRF table does not say who is a mentor-IIT faculty member, so no "Mentor faculty from IIT X" label could be given). Not current in the last NIRF file and not on any live list -> former (or visiting with range).
* joining_year = earliest NIRF joining date (any year); NIRF dates can be regularisation dates, and for 2019-era rows "Other/Contractual" start dates mean visiting appointments. Blank when NIRF has no row (wb-only or directory-only people), never guessed. First archived appearance is used only for FIRST in the range, not for joining_year.
* FIRST = min(NIRF join year, first archived-list year, first NIRF year). LAST = NIRF leaving-date year if present, else max(last NIRF year with currently-working = Yes (the 2019 file counts as 2018), last archived-list year). NIRF "No" with no leaving date: last Yes year (uncertain by up to 1 year). Archived lists after the 2020-22 reorganisation are absent, so LAST for 2019-2022 leavers who left before the first NIRF 2021 table and are not in it may be missing (known gap).
* Stale pages: archived school lists were treated as evidence only up to the NIRF leaving date when one exists (e.g. Dileep AD listed to 2022-08 on archived pages, NIRF leave 2023).

## Size check
NIRF 2026: 205 currently working (189 Regular, 15 Contractual, 1 Other) of 226 records. Roster: current 216 + 56 visiting/adjunct. All 189 NIRF-Regular current people are in the roster as current; 188 current rows are NIRF-2026-confirmed (plus visiting/adjunct ones). The 28 current rows without any NIRF record are mostly joiners after the Feb 2026 submission and directory-only people (including 5 CAIR/IKSMHA/CQST people with other schools); the NIRF totals also omit some people, so the roster being 27 above NIRF-Regular is expected. The institute's own annual report 2023-24 says "over 350 faculty members" (includes all teaching staff types); not comparable.
