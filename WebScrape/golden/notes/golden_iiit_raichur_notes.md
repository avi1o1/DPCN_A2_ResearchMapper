## Why small
IIIT Raichur began teaching c.2019 and nearly all current faculty joined 2021-2025. The tenure rule removes most listed papers, which predate joining. The faculty pages list publications for only 6 of 15 people; the institute "Publications Explorer" (/research/publications) is JavaScript-driven and renders 0 items without JS (it only aggregates the profile pages anyway). No annual report before 2022-23 has a publication list. No years 2016-2021 are covered: no person was at the institute with a paper in that window that satisfies the rules. This is a structural bias (the set is 2022-2026 only, 25 of 39 rows from 2023-2024).
