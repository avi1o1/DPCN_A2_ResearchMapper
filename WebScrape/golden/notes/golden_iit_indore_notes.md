## Rules applied
Tenure: joining year from roster (own page / NIRF); paper counts only if year strictly after joining year (medium). arXiv rows high. Per-person cap 8 across all sources; lists newest-first sampled at even spacing (CSE) or taken as shown (hand rows). Dedup by normalised title.

## Rejected / skipped
